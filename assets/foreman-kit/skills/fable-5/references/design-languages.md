# Named design languages

The gallery names its aesthetic before building — 100+ distinct named design languages appear across the corpus. A name is a *compression* of a full design decision: palette, type personality, texture, and motion character all fall out of it. Pick the closest family below, adapt it, and **coin a fresh two-or-three-word name** for the specific project.

Format per family: the mood, its palette signature, its type signature, its texture/motion signature, and real example names harvested from the gallery.

---

## 1. Warm Editorial / Paper
The gallery's workhorse. Feels like a well-set print magazine on warm stock.
- **Palette:** bone/paper background (`#F2F0EB`, `#F8F7F7`, `#FAF9F6`), near-black ink (`#0D0D0D`, `#1D1D1D`), one warm accent — ember/vermilion/amber (`#FF3B2F`, `#E4572E`, `#D97706`). Muted text = ink at 55-65%.
- **Type:** grotesk display (Bricolage Grotesque, Space Grotesk, Inter Tight) or an editorial serif (Fraunces, Instrument Serif, Playfair) for headlines; uppercase eyebrows with wide tracking.
- **Texture/motion:** hairline rules, grain overlay, coordinate/word tickers, unhurried parallax and reveals.
- **Examples:** Warm Cartography, Warm Clay Editorial, Warm Editorial Ember, Warm Paper Studio, Soft Editorial Bento, Quiet Craft Editorial, Marmalade Editorial, Ink & Vermilion Editorial, Wabi Editorial, Millstone Craft.

## 2. Swiss / Brutalist / Kinetic
Confident, structural, grid-forward; type as architecture.
- **Palette:** high-contrast mono (paper + ink) or stark black/white with one electric accent (lime `#DCF986`, red, cobalt).
- **Type:** huge grotesque, tight negative tracking, `line-height < 0.9`, often uppercase; per-letter spans for kinetic motion.
- **Texture/motion:** exposed grid lines, registration marks, magnetic/recoiling letters, hard cuts, marquees.
- **Examples:** Monocrit, Kinetic Brutalism, Electric Brutalism, Drafting-Table Brutalism, Warm Brutalist Grid, Floating-Pill Neo-Brutalism, Grid Monument, Neo-Structuralist Blueprint, Quant Brutalist Editorial.

## 3. Clinical / Precision / Engineering SaaS
Bright, authoritative, "well-lit control room on clean paper." Trust through restraint.
- **Palette:** white/bone canvas, charcoal ink, one decisive accent (electric lime, cobalt), status green `#22C55E` for live dots, hairlines at 6-10% black.
- **Type:** Inter / Inter Tight / Geist; precise spacing; mono for data (Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono).
- **Texture/motion:** faint hairline grid that fades out, live "console" product mockups, count-up numbers, calm reveals.
- **Examples:** Precision Grid, Clinical Calm, Clinical Serenity, Clinical Warmth, Daylight Workbench, Workbench Editorial, Paper Circuit, Bone & Volt, Putty & Voltage.

## 4. Glass / Liquid Glass / Luminous
Depth through translucency and light. iOS-adjacent, premium.
- **Palette:** dark or photographic background, frosted white surfaces (`rgba(255,255,255,0.06-0.12)`), soft inner/outer glow, one luminous accent.
- **Type:** clean sans (Inter, DM Sans, Geist); restrained.
- **Texture/motion:** backdrop blur, refraction/specular highlights, floating pill navs, drifting color blobs, gentle parallax over video.
- **Examples:** Liquid Glass, Bloom Liquid Glass, Equilibrium, Lumen Aurora, Astral Veil, Halo, Spectral Orbit.

## 5. Cinematic / Noir / Dark
Full-viewport, video- or shader-driven, moody.
- **Palette:** near-black grounds, desaturated photography/video, a single cold or blood accent; timecode/scan-line details.
- **Type:** condensed display or elegant serif; small uppercase meta with timecodes.
- **Texture/motion:** looping background video (often HLS/Mux), progressive multi-layer bottom blur, self-drawing SVG, character-by-character headline reveals, scan lines.
- **Examples:** Cinematic Brutalist Noir, Red Noir, Spectral Darkroom, Obsidian Lacquer, Obsidian & Champagne, Midnight Atelier, Atelier Noir, Institutional Crypto Terminal.

## 6. Heritage / Craft / Apothecary / Nature
Earthy, tactile, slow. Artisanal and organic.
- **Palette:** forest/olive/clay/sand earth tones, cream paper, brass/gold accents.
- **Type:** sculptural serif (Fraunces, Playfair) paired with a humanist sans; generous leading.
- **Texture/motion:** paper grain, botanical imagery, soft dissolves, unhurried scroll.
- **Examples:** Forest Apothecary, Verdant Apothecary, Archival Apothecary, Heritage Verdant, Forest & Gold, Sun-Fired Earth, Soft Heritage Edit, Sculptural Serif Atelier, Inkpress Atelier, Culinary Architecture, Tide to Table.

## 7. Terminal / Quant / Technical Mono
Data-dense, monospace, "trade desk / dossier."
- **Palette:** dark or phosphor-green on black, or mono-on-paper; amber/green data accents.
- **Type:** monospace throughout (Geist Mono, JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono, Space Mono); tabular numerals.
- **Texture/motion:** live tickers, blinking cursors, scramble/typewriter text, count-ups, grid readouts.
- **Examples:** Monochrome Terminal Editorial, Phosphor Olive, Cold War Dossier, Paper Circuit, Electric Stone, Voltmark.

## 8. Quiet Luxury / Gallery Minimalism
Maximum restraint; space is the material.
- **Palette:** near-monochrome, warm neutrals, whisper-quiet contrast, at most one muted accent.
- **Type:** refined serif or a single elegant grotesque; enormous whitespace.
- **Texture/motion:** almost none — slow fades, a single considered gesture; the calm *is* the design.
- **Examples:** Quiet Luxury Consulting, Quiet Luxury Wellness, Quiet Sanctuary, Quiet Pastel Block-Grid, Gallery Minimalism, Gallery Arch, Soft Editorial Minimalism, Velvet Precision.

## Coining a new name (do this every project)

1. **Pick a material or place + a quality:** *Molten Paper*, *Electric Stone*, *Warm Cartography*, *Drafting-Table Brutalism*. Concrete noun + evocative modifier.
2. **Make it imply the palette and texture.** If the name doesn't tell you the colors and the feel, it's too vague.
3. **Two or three words.** Longer is a tagline, not a design language.
4. **State the ONE big idea** alongside it — the single distinctive move (cursor-as-image-card, headline that recoils, shader that reacts to scroll).

A name you can't derive a palette + type + motion from is not doing its job. When unsure between directions, propose 2-3 named options to the user with a one-line signature each and let them choose.
